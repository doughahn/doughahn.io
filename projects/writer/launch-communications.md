### Launching a national brand

In close collaboration with the COO, marketing director, art director, external PR agencies, and construction firms, I played a central role in crafting executive speeches, designing key talking points for press releases, and shaping media narratives for the unveiling of a major real estate development project for Bently Heritage.

By placing a special emphasis on the preservation of historic buildings, we generated positive media engagement and public sentiment, positioning the company as an institution that valued its historical roots while also being a catalyst for local economic growth.

### Speechwriting

This excerpt is from the distillery&rsquo;s groundbreaking ceremony. The goal was to get stakeholders on the client&rsquo;s side.

> What I want is a product that this community loves; this product is about you. You should be able to buy it without breaking the bank. We will be a high quality product, but still something you enjoy on your porch with the people you care about.
>
> If Minden doesn&rsquo;t love what we&rsquo;re creating, I&rsquo;ll toss it out and make something better. And once we&rsquo;ve refined our product with pride, I want to remind the world that great things start in Minden.

### Press releases

I have written dozens of press releases over the years, which have earned placement in target publications. My strategy is always to make the journalist&rsquo;s job easier, using industry-standard structure and style and keeping releases around 350 words.

The release announcing the distillery was picked up by 61 media outlets, representing an estimated 8 million earned media impressions with an advertising equivalent of about $80K. It was followed by three media inquiries from notable publications. Here is how it opened:

> ##### Distillery to be one of first in state under new law
> Christopher Bently, a local entrepreneur and businessman, announced that he is converting the Minden Flour Milling Company building, a National Registered Historic Place, into a craft distillery named Nevada Heritage.
>
> The new distillery will be one of the first operating in the state since Assembly Bill 153 was passed earlier this year, which allows local distilleries to operate.
