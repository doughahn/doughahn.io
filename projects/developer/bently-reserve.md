### A brand for a historic venue

The Bently Reserve is the Old Federal Reserve Building of San Francisco, now an event venue. I first created a scalable, visual style guide for it. The project was driven by internal needs as well as by third parties and PR agencies that needed accessible style rules and logo assets.

![A sample from the Bently Reserve brand book](/reserve-brand-book-sample.webp)

To create an effective style guide, I helped achieve internal consensus on key issues, such as the brand name and voice, which had been in contention in recent years. I facilitated meetings with key leaders and conducted polls to understand the key issues and propose a vision everyone could agree upon.

> #### BRAND DNA
> We empower innovative and sophisticated experiences by setting the stage for perfectly executed meetings, once-in-a-lifetime events, and unforgettable weddings.

The book helped the internal design team and became the foundation for the company&rsquo;s search marketing campaigns, guiding the brand&rsquo;s digital presence for years to come.

### Content control in the venue&rsquo;s hands

I was the developer and writer for the venue&rsquo;s new website, working closely with key stakeholders. We built it on the Craft CMS, which I tailored with bespoke content models for the client&rsquo;s needs, putting content management into the marketing team&rsquo;s hands.

![One of the venue&rsquo;s spaces, showing a clear call to action and key features based on user intent](/bently-reserve-banking-hall.webp)

#### Pivoting to conferences

A core part of the project was a pivot from weddings toward a business and conference center. We emphasized the building&rsquo;s history: many of its boardrooms were where the original members of the Federal Reserve&rsquo;s Board of Directors met. The client also partnered with SFMOMA&rsquo;s Artist Gallery Program to bring a modern sense of design to the space.

![Meeting spaces for the Bently Reserve](/reserve-spaces.webp)

In the CMS, I created entries that fed several places on the site, with customizable capacities, image galleries, room features, and layout configurations.

![A room page for the Bently Reserve](/reserve-room.webp)

#### One simple goal

The site&rsquo;s core strategy was to generate quality leads. We iterated on the RFP form, A/B testing various configurations, and arrived at a simplified form that drove quality leads. We integrated it with Salesforce CRM to help the sales team build relationships and convert leads into sales.

![A section of the final RFP form, with simplified, user-friendly UX](/reserve-rfp.webp)
