### Rowing on San Francisco Bay

I joined the Embarcadero Rowing Club's board in 2023 to lead recruitment. The club had about 18 members and was thinking of selling boats; the Learn to Row program I built helped make it the largest club in the Bay Area Whaleboat Rowing Association, with more than 70 members. Today I chair the board. I also [designed and coded the club's website](/projects/designed-and-coded-a-heritage-driven-website-for-a-bay-area-rowing-club/) and [built its event and race app](/projects/built-a-club-event-and-race-app-that-has-handled-thousands-of-signups/), which has handled more than 4,000 signups.

For the Bay Area Whaleboat Rowing Association, I'm a board member and the volunteer developer. I built [its public website](/projects/built-a-website-for-a-historic-rowing-association-with-ai-and-human-art-direction/) and [its regatta scoring and operations platform](/projects/rebuilt-regatta-scoring-and-operations-for-a-multi-club-rowing-association/), and I [coordinate the safety fleet](/projects/built-a-safe-fleet-for-whaleboats-on-the-bay/) of motorized vessels that supports its races.

### Poetry

I founded [*Sink Review*](/projects/designing-and-developing-a-digital-poetry-magazine-for-18-years/), an online poetry journal focused on typography, in 2006, and have edited, designed, and developed it since, across 19 issues.

### Community

1. With the Project Management Institute's San Francisco Bay Area chapter, I [build community events for its sustainability program](/projects/building-sustainable-community-with-fellow-project-managers/), both virtual and in person.
1. From 2018 to 2022, I [fostered an inclusive tabletop gaming community](/projects/fostered-an-inclusive-gaming-community/) in San Francisco, recruiting and training volunteers to run free public games at local shops.
